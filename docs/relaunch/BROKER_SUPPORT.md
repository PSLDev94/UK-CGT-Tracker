# Broker export qualification

The review build has nine tested ordinary-share workflows across six brokers. Trading 212, Freetrade and HL have independently published file evidence for specified layouts. The configured IBKR execution layout and the assisted ii and AJ Bell layouts have synthetic test coverage. Wider compatibility requires representative broker-issued statements and reconciled contract notes. [Acceptance matrix](REVIEW_READY.md#broker-acceptance-matrix) · [Fixture register](../../tests/fixtures/brokers/README.md)

### Native preview contract

All readers require an explicit ordinary-share confirmation, saved with the draft and checked again at commit. A valid ISIN alone does not establish tax classification. The accepted trade retains the original native fields and adapter version in provenance. Exact known headers may be reordered; unknown, duplicate, missing or added columns do not get silently dropped.

Freetrade reconciles account-currency prices, instrument-currency gross amounts, base/effective exchange rates, stamp duty and explicit FX fees to net cash. The supplied GBP rate must exclude the separately counted fee; the exported fee percentage and amount must reconcile on both purchases and sales. Trading 212 supports GBP/GBX prices, separately denominated GBP dealing/conversion fees and verified foreign-currency rates where the cash total is GBP. Broker-reported `Result` is never used as UK taxable gain. HL/ii/AJ Bell require contract-note evidence and reconcile quantity × price ± verified fees to net cash. IBKR requires execution-level stock rows and reconciles signed proceeds + commissions + taxes = net cash. [HMRC allowable dealing costs](https://www.gov.uk/tax-sell-shares/work-out-your-gain).

The T212 and Freetrade layouts timestamp executions in UTC; the adapters convert to Europe/London before selecting the trade date. Order/execution IDs are account-scoped. A repeated trade ID inside one native file requires review, even if the two rows look identical. Across imports, an identical normalized source row is a duplicate; changed fields under that ID are a conflict. Never use an amount-only fingerprint to collapse fills.

Freetrade monthly-statement and tax-certificate notifications are counted as explicit non-transaction exclusions only when every financial/execution field is blank; a notification carrying amounts or quantities blocks review. A file with any unresolved record cannot be partially saved. Free shares, zero-value trades, splits, transfers and unknown event types are not reclassified as ordinary purchases or ignored. [Freetrade explicitly warns that provider transfers and some corporate actions are absent from its Activity Feed](https://help.freetrade.io/en/articles/6627909-what-information-is-included-in-the-csv-download-of-my-activity-feed); successful parsing is not evidence of complete acquisition history. Check account records and do not delete unsupported rows to force acceptance.

## What verified support means

Each enabled adapter has a version, exact recognised headers/section structure, transaction-code mapping, date convention, currency and amount semantics, supported account/asset scope and reviewed fixtures. Cover buys/sells, fees/taxes, GBP/foreign currency, fractions, multiple executions, duplicates/overlaps, zero values, multi-year history and unsupported activity. Verify parser totals against the original export and calculation totals independently.

Collect current and historical anonymised samples with permission. Prefer official or openly licensed samples when suitable. Preserve schema and financial relationships during anonymisation. Never commit unredacted customer data. Limited preview adapters require documented schema evidence and tests. General certification additionally requires representative exports, reconciliation and independent review; otherwise state the exact supported subset and return clear review requirements.

Local intake directory: `.private/broker-samples/` (Git-ignored). Keep original customer exports there until anonymisation and permission review are complete. Only approved synthetic/anonymised fixtures belong in `tests/fixtures`.

100% support means every record in the explicitly certified format is accounted for and every supported trade is correctly normalised. It cannot honestly mean every future broker format or every asset type. Unknown columns, changed transaction codes and unsupported events must be caught rather than guessed.

## Canonical interchange format

The currently implemented format is an internal template, not a native broker CSV. The user or a verified adapter must provide these exact columns, in any order:

`Transaction ID,Trade date,Type,ISIN,Quantity,Consideration,Fees,Currency,GBP per unit,Asset type`

- Transaction ID: stable execution identifier within the selected broker/account. Separate executions retain separate IDs even when economics match.
- Trade date: ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; no inferred UK/US date order.
- Type: `BUY` or `SELL`. Explicit `CASH_DEPOSIT`, `CASH_WITHDRAWAL`, `CASH_DIVIDEND` and `CASH_INTEREST` are counted as exclusions from share gains. Other codes require review.
- ISIN: uppercase identifier with a valid check digit. Instrument classification must also be confirmed; ISIN validity alone does not prove ordinary-share status.
- Quantity: positive decimal shares, up to 12 decimal places. Never encode the side using a negative quantity.
- Consideration: gross trade consideration excluding the separately specified fees, in major source-currency units. Do not paste a net cash amount without converting it to this contract.
- Fees: non-negative allowable incidental transaction costs in the same source currency; enter `0` when none. Fee eligibility must be checked by the adapter/tax specification.
- Currency: supported ISO currency code. `GBX` and pence are rejected; convert explicitly to GBP first.
- GBP per unit: the number of pounds per one major source-currency unit, using the applicable trade-date conversion; exactly `1` for GBP. No reciprocal guessing.
- Asset type: `ORDINARY_SHARE` in this first increment. Other classifications require explicit handling.

Decimal points are `.` with no currency symbols, grouping separators or exponent notation. CSV quoting and UTF-8 BOMs are supported. Binary/control characters other than tabs and line endings are rejected. JSON transport overhead is allowed separately from the decoded file-size cap. The limit is 10 MiB and 100,000 data records. Original monetary values, currency and exchange rate are retained in provenance. Converted GBP values retain the full product of the original amount and rate, up to 24 decimal places; reporting rounds only at the documented output boundary.

The preview classifies all parsed data records as accepted, excluded, duplicate or needs review. A single unresolved record blocks committing the entire file. Duplicate IDs with conflicting contents are flagged, not overwritten. An all-duplicate file is a no-op requiring an explanatory result, never a successful new import.

## Further broker qualification — 17 November 2026

- AJ Bell: separate exact header `Date,Description,Transaction,Quantity,Price,Amount (GBP)`, recorded during the 17 November review of [Sharesight’s AJ Bell integration guide](https://www.sharesight.com/uk/partners/aj-bell/). The guide’s suggestions to infer tickers/rates and omit other records are not used.
- Freetrade: exact aliases `Total Amount in Account Currency` and `Total Amount in Instrument Currency` are recognised, without accepting additional or ambiguous columns. Foreign FX fees use the exported base rate, effective rate, basis points and explicit GBP fee. Buy fees increase cost; sell fees reduce proceeds. A synthetic £1,000 gross acquisition with £4.50 fee, followed by a half-sale with £600 gross and £2.70 fee, produces £95.05 gain. Changing any rate, fee or net total blocks the file. Source references and limits are in the fixture README.
- A cumulative import compares the broker row and its financial details with the saved execution. A revised contract-note description or an old unit selection that the broker reader ignored can still match the original trade. The original transaction retains its evidence, and the new receipt records the revised reference. A change to the exported row, the verified amounts or a unit used in the calculation requires review. The comparison also recognises the older fingerprints stored in PostgreSQL.
- `broker-qualification.test.ts` applies record accounting, malformed fields, cash mismatch, unknown activity, column order/quoting, duplicate/conflict and combined matching checks to each fixture path. Generated fractional trades compare against integer-pence expectations. The persisted workspace and PostgreSQL qualification suites run upload → evidence → commit → calculation → snapshot → duplicate retry → reversal for the same corpus.

### Execution identity and earlier imports

Each IBKR account label binds to one statement ClientAccountID in a portfolio. Use `ClientAccountID:TradeID` from the Flex statement as the Transaction ID when starting with the template. After a native or prefixed template trade has been saved, the raw TradeID on its receipt also works on that account. Two distinct physical accounts may reuse the same TradeID. Reversing the last active execution releases the label. Identical native/template economics count as duplicates. Conflicting values need review. [IBKR Trades Flex fields](https://www.ibkrguides.com/reportingreference/reportguide/tradesfq.htm)

Older IBKR receipts with ambiguous account assignments, overlapping executions or unevidenced account-base conversion hold calculation and report publication until reviewed. Reverse the affected receipt and re-import the original correctly identified executions. Older canonical receipts with excluded cash records lack their original excluded fields, so their original templates must be rechecked. No financial input is inferred while performing these checks.

### Cash-only records and nil consideration

In the template, deposits, withdrawals and interest can carry a cash amount and its currency. Leave the ISIN and asset type empty. Set share quantity and dealing fees to zero or leave them empty. A cash dividend may name its share by valid ISIN and ordinary-share classification. Native dividend files may also report the eligible share count. A quantity acquired through a reinvestment needs a separate evidenced purchase.

An ordinary buy or sale requires positive gross consideration. A gift, transfer or negligible-value claim can require different treatment. Obtain specialist advice before recording it. Use a documented positive tax value only when the adviser confirms that the ordinary-share template represents that event and verifies the basis, date and costs. Keep genuine nil-basis and other specialist cases outside completed reports until handled appropriately. [HMRC market-value guidance](https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/market-value)

## Rechecking receipts and overlapping statements

A repeated execution reference within a single template holds that file. Obtain the fill-level identifiers or a documented aggregate containing the exact total quantity, proceeds and fees of that order. A cumulative statement can include saved executions alongside new ones. The preview compares the financial terms and identifies conflicting fields when a template and a native statement disagree.

For an older receipt with unverified cash exclusions or ambiguous same-file duplicate rows, upload the identical original statement under the same account. Re-enter any required statement details. When every execution reconciles and the latest reader validates every excluded entry, select **Verify original receipt** and recalculate. This records the recheck without adding trades. Where a row still needs review, resolve it against the broker evidence and replace the affected receipt through reversal and re-import. New receipts retain the financial fields used to exclude cash activity.

For an overlap between account labels, first check whether the wrong account was selected for a cumulative statement. Correct the selection or reverse an incorrect receipt. A support reviewer can clear a genuine coincidence only after checking two original statements identifying separate taxable accounts and verifying the underlying fills and fees. Record the evidence in the controlled case file. Keep personal identifiers in that file. Use the existing administrative database connection and the saved receipt ID shown in the overlap message.

```sh
node scripts/review-statement-overlap.mjs --draft DRAFT_ID --other OTHER_RECEIPT
node scripts/review-statement-overlap.mjs --draft DRAFT_ID --other OTHER_RECEIPT --case CASE_REF --evidence SOURCE_REF --verified-separate-accounts --apply
```

Use `--receipt EXISTING_RECEIPT` instead of `--draft` when checking a historical overlap. The decision covers the exact file, target account and other receipt. Changed files and further overlaps are checked again. A customer-facing login has read access only. A reviewer can withdraw an incorrect decision using its exact account ID, file digest and other receipt ID in an administrative transaction, then recheck affected reports. IBKR continues to use the broker’s ClientAccountID and TradeID.

### Verifying a dividend that resembles an execution

A Trading 212 dividend carrying an identifier, share quantity and unit price can look like an ordinary purchase or sale. The reader checks whether positive withholding, its currency and the distribution per share explain the actual sterling payment. If the tax field is blank, zero, unreadable, denominated in an unexplained currency or too small to distinguish the cash from a gross execution, the file needs evidence. Check the dividend voucher, credited cash and any separate reinvestment against the statement. A genuine cash dividend without execution-shaped fields continues through the regular exclusion path. Keep unresolved shares and changes in capital with the case until the treatment is established.
