# CGT Tracker end-to-end assessment

19 November 2026 · Railway staging · release `5193b64` · calculation engine `2.0.0-alpha.4`

**Keep paid launch on hold.** The ordinary-share matching engine agreed with an independently implemented model across 39,735 disposals. Four high-impact cases produced a completed report with the wrong result. A cash label hid an execution, assigning a statement to two account labels counted it twice, the template discarded a genuine fill, and a private family sale used the wrong tax basis. These deserve the first engineering work. The earlier fixes for historical basis, exact pool allocation, IBKR account identity and stale report links were present in this build. [Staging](https://web-staging-e003bc3b.up.railway.app/) · [Passing CI run for this commit](https://github.com/PSLDev94/UK-CGT-Tracker/actions/runs/44438808782)

## 1. Fixes that protect published financial results

| Priority | What happened | Required change and acceptance test |
|---|---|---|
| **1. A cash label can silently remove a sale.** Trading 212 and Freetrade | Buy 10 shares at £1, sell two at £2, then sell three at £5. The executions imply a **£14 gain**. With the last row labelled Deposit or TOP_UP and its execution details still present, the native readers excluded it. The saved result was **£2**. The Trading 212 CSV and both paid PDFs served that figure as complete. Both Trading 212 layouts and the Freetrade layout reproduced the exclusion. | Check the broker fields before excluding each type of cash activity. Hold the entire file for evidence when a deposit or top-up carries contradictory dealing details. Keep genuine deposits, interest and dividend entitlements usable. Test a genuine dividend with a share count, each contradictory fixture, the save path, and eligibility for all three downloads. Locate receipts produced by affected parser versions. Where source fields were discarded, request the statement before clearing the report. [Native readers](../../src/domain/imports/native.ts) · [Receipt integrity checks](../../src/server/import-integrity.ts) |
| **2. A second account label can double a portfolio.** Five brokers | Saving the identical statement under two same-broker GIA labels changed a Trading 212 gain from **£5.25 to £10.50**, Freetrade from **£74.37 to £148.75**, and HL, Interactive Investor and AJ Bell from **£70.88 to £141.75** each. Per-disposal rounding explains the penny differences from an exact doubling. IBKR held the duplicate. | Compare statement fingerprints and overlapping executions across same-broker labels in the taxpayer’s portfolio, both at preview and under the save lock. Show which receipt overlaps. Support genuinely separate accounts with corroborating account evidence. Test identical, partly overlapping and non-overlapping exports, two simultaneous saves, reversal and re-import. Screen historical receipts and withdraw affected publications until resolved. [Workspace import checks](../../src/server/workspace.ts) · [Broker reproductions](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/brokers/findings.json) |
| **3. The template can silently discard a second fill of the same order.** | A Trading 212 order contained two distinct executions one minute apart, each selling two shares at £2 after a purchase at £1 per share. The native preview correctly held the repeated order ID for clarification. The template retained only the date, treated the second fill as an already-included row on the very first import, and published a complete **£2 gain**. The two fills give **£4**. | Distinguish repetition inside a file from replay against a saved execution. Hold ambiguous repetitions until the user provides fill-level references or documented aggregation accounting for every fill and fee. Keep a genuine re-upload idempotent. Test two identical-looking fills, a genuine duplicated line, a documented aggregation and a cumulative export. Include prior templates with same-file duplicates in the impact assessment. [Template review](../../src/domain/imports/review.ts) · [Native versus template evidence](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/brokers/ambiguous-fills.json) |
| **4. A connected-party sale can turn a gain into a reported loss.** | A UK-resident individual bought shares for £400 and privately sold them to their adult child for £100 when the evidenced market value was £1,000. Assuming no other relief or costs, the applicable gain is **£600**. Every onscreen declaration could be answered truthfully. The template produced a **£300 loss** and enabled purchase. The portfolio had sufficient recorded shares. | Ask about private, connected-party and other transactions requiring a special basis before treating statement consideration as the tax basis. Route affected cases to review and keep purchase unavailable until the treatment is evidenced. Exercise a market sale, a positive-value family sale, and a case involving a relief or transfer. Record the scope declaration with the published result and have a qualified UK reviewer approve the handling of prior declarations. [Customer declaration](../../src/ui/workspace.tsx) · [HMRC on connected persons](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14560) · [HMRC market-value guidance](https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/market-value) |
| **5. Sterling conversion can round twice.** | A purchase of $451.66665 at £0.81300003 per dollar costs £367.2049999999995. A subsequent £400 sale should show a £367.20 cost and **£32.80 gain** under the app’s penny convention. The saved sterling cost reaches £367.205 before reporting, which produces **£32.79**. A second independent input produced the same one-penny error. | Preserve the amount and supplied FX rate through matching and apply the approved rounding rule at the reporting boundary. If the storage model cannot represent the conversion, hold the case explicitly. Record the foreign figures, rate and GBP result in exportable evidence. Test both sides of a half-penny, fees, a loss, pooled and partial sales, and stored legacy conversions. [Conversion code](../../src/domain/imports/review.ts) · [Saved example](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/engine-current/sterlingRoundTrip.csv) · [HMRC on foreign-currency transactions](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg78310) |
| **6. A report can mix two revisions while downloading.** | A synthetic customer started an evidence download and reversed the receipt in another session while the entitlement check was pending. The request returned HTTP 200, a **£30 gain** and a complete label. Both source entries were reduced to a transaction hash. The normal download for that receipt included the broker, file row and original execution reference. Opening the same link afterwards correctly returned 404. | Read the published result and its source details from one consistent revision, or return a refresh message if that revision changes. Require each expected source to resolve before sending the evidence. With this ordering, the download must contain the complete original receipt details or clearly ask for recalculation. Cover CSV and PDF, ordinary reversal and replacement-import paths. [Download handler](../../src/server/reports-api.ts) · [Overlapping download text](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/reports/overlapping-download.txt) |

## 2. Fixes for broker recovery, calculations and customer guidance

| Priority | Customer impact | Required change and acceptance test |
|---|---|---|
| **7. Preserve small fractional quantities in native imports.** | Each of the six native broker paths tested accepted 0.000001 shares but rejected 0.0000001 after converting it internally to exponent notation. The same buy at £100,000 per share and sale at £200,000 per share worked through the template and returned the expected **£0.01 gain**. Trading 212 documents seven-place orders at that price level. | Pass fixed-point values between adapters and the shared validator. Test the native and template routes with quantities just below and above the formatting boundary, up to each broker’s documented precision. Retain rejection of unsupported precision. [Raw results](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/brokers/more.json) · [Trading 212 fractional-order precision](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/9511997937437-Can-I-trade-with-fractional-shares) |
| **8. Allow evidenced overlaps between a native statement and the template.** | On five non-IBKR brokers, a template containing a previously saved, economically identical execution and one new sale blocked the whole file. It described the old execution as having different values. IBKR correctly recognised the duplicate and accepted the new row. This frustrates the very recovery path offered for difficult files. | Compare account, execution identity, instrument, side, date, quantity, original currency, consideration, fees and supported conversions. Show remaining differences when equivalence cannot be proven. Test both upload orders, changed financial terms, partial overlaps and retries. An actual conflict must continue to block. [Import comparison](../../src/domain/imports/review.ts) · [Broker results](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/brokers/findings.json) |
| **9. Separate uncertain historical holdings from this year’s evidenced disposals.** | Security A was bought in 2000 and partly sold in 2005, then had no disposals in the selected year. Security B cost £100 and sold for £150 in that year. The CSV got B’s **£50 gain** right, but A’s unresolved history suppressed the annual total and prevented purchase. The control with a current-year sale of A correctly blocked. | Track which security and period each issue affects. Have the tax reviewer approve when the year’s disposals can be completed with an explicitly unresolved, separate pool. Show the outstanding A history and withhold its valuation. Keep the block when A's basis can affect a selected-year sale or when account-level history itself is incomplete. [Engine](../../src/domain/cgt/calculate.ts) · [Persisted controls](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/engine-current/persist.json) |
| **10. Distinguish tax pools, shares actually held and unknown balances.** | Selling all ten shares on 3 April and buying ten back on 23 April correctly produced a **£100 gain** and a tax pool of ten shares at 5 April. The investor owned zero shares on that date. The holdings scenario nevertheless labelled ten as shares at the snapshot and valued them at £150 for a £15 price. Separately, buy one, sell two with missing history, then buy twelve. The paid draft correctly withheld the gain but said **“No remaining pool balances.”** A genuinely closed pool used the same wording. | Label the Section 104 quantity explicitly. A scenario advertised as a holding valuation needs the physical balance on the stated date, or an explanation that only a tax-pool illustration is available. Identify each unresolved pool in the screen, CSV and evidence PDF, even where other pools are valid. Test the April repurchase, blocked history with later purchases, a genuinely closed holding and a mixed portfolio. [Scenario screen](../../src/ui/restored-harvesting.tsx) · [Draft evidence](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/reports/unknown-pool-evidence.txt) · [HMRC matching examples](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51560) |
| **11. Reject a cutoff before the selected tax year.** | Selecting 2024–25 with history through 31 December 2023 created a £0 draft and a no-disposals message despite an imported August 2024 sale. Checkout correctly blocked it. No part of the selected year had been assessed. | Validate the lower bound on the form and server and preserve the user's chosen tax year when correcting it. Test the day before 6 April, 6 April itself, partial-year evidence, the following 30 days and future dates. [Calculation entry point](../../src/server/workspace.ts) · [Draft CSV](../../.private/reassessment-5193b64/engine-current/priorYearCutoff.csv) |
| **12. Use the right precision for money and per-share figures.** | On both hosted WebKit profiles, 5.25 shares costing £5.25 at a scenario price of £1.00095238 showed **£5.26 value and £0.01 difference**. The exact value is £5.254999995, which displays as **£5.25 and £0.00**. In the public Section 104 tool, 1,000 shares at £0.004 correctly cost £4, but the average cost per share displayed as **£0.00**. | Round monetary totals once from full precision. Give unit prices meaningful sub-penny precision or display pence per share. Apply the same convention to the public tool, calculation detail and holdings scenario. Test positive, negative and exact half-penny boundaries, fractional prices and a normal two-decimal price. [Scenario result](../../.private/fresh-financial-assessment/journey/hosted/edge-ux.json) · [Unit-price result](../../.private/fresh-financial-assessment/journey/hosted/unit-values.json) |
| **13. Correct the tax guide’s instructions for income and losses.** | The rates guide starts with “taxable income” and then tells readers to deduct a fixed £12,570. A reader who already has post-allowance taxable income could deduct it twice. For £36,000 of post-allowance taxable income and £10,000 of taxable gains in 2025–26, the illustrated simple case yields **£2,298**. A second allowance deduction suggests **£1,800**. The interactive checker uses the correct starting point. The guide also omits the need to claim allowable losses. | Align the guide with the checker. State whether an example starts with gross or post-allowance income, use the individual’s applicable allowance, and explain the conditions for carrying forward losses. Have the worked example and loss wording checked independently. In the same edit, give the actual rate changes from 10% to 18% and 20% to 24%, and correct the public Section 104 tool’s claim that it uses the same arithmetic library as the workspace. ## 5. Release sequence

1. **Protect existing reports first.** Add fixtures for items 1–6 and a read-only impact assessment by parser version, overlapping receipt, conversion precision and recorded scope. Separate definite matches from cases needing the original statement or a new user declaration. Preserve original receipts and report revisions. Withhold affected or unverified reports, explain the reason, and regenerate them only after the data or scope is confirmed.
2. **Qualify the fixes throughout the product.** Run each numerical regression from original file through saved transactions, calculation, CSV and both PDFs. Repeat the real file-picker, assisted correction and download flows on desktop and mobile WebKit. Include genuine separate broker accounts, reversed receipts, non-subscribers and existing subscribers. Re-run the independent portfolio model and PostgreSQL suite.
3. **Obtain independent tax and broker evidence.** A UK CGT professional should review matching, rounding, reviewed opening balances, separated historical issues and special-basis exclusions. Request the original broker documents listed above. The evidence here supports personal, UK-resident, ordinary-share histories within the tested rules. Derivatives, shorts, reorganisations, employment awards and other specialist cases require explicit handling. [Tax qualification scope](TAX_REVIEW.md)
4. **Finish the operational rehearsals before taking money.** Complete a provider-delivered dispute and the full clock-driven renewal, decline, authentication and recovery sequence on one mapped sandbox customer, checking the paid PDFs after each transition. Establish ownership for any legacy paid customers. Rehearse production-equivalent encrypted backup, restore, retention and external alert delivery with agreed operators. Review customer-facing business, privacy, cancellation and subscription disclosures before enabling real checkout. These are release gates already recorded by the project. [Billing](BILLING.md) · [Backups](BACKUP_RECOVERY.md) · [Release checklist](REVIEW_READY.md) · [GOV.UK distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses)

The tests and synthetic reproductions used staging or isolated local databases. The randomized comparison starts from verified GBP transactions, so it cannot validate broker ingestion, unstated personal circumstances, foreign consideration or filing eligibility. No customer purchase, production deployment or DNS change was part of this assessment.

## Implementation following this assessment

The findings above record the reproductions on `5193b64`. Engine `2.0.0-alpha.5` requires the new ordinary-market declaration, checks FX evidence at the saved precision, and distinguishes historical pool uncertainty from incomplete disposal costs. Download requests now read their calculation and source references together. The updated broker workflow distinguishes ambiguous fills, replays, cash exclusions and overlaps between accounts. The original receipt can be checked again without adding its trades a second time. The scenario, reports, cutoff validator and public guides use the revised rules.
