# UK ordinary-share CGT rules and calculation assessment

Reviewed 19 November 2026. CGT Tracker calculates realised sterling gains and losses for one individual investing in ordinary shares in the same personal capacity, using taxable accounts, complete trade history and the ordinary acquisition-cost basis. The investor must be UK resident throughout the relevant history, including later repurchases. An individual's tax bill also depends on income, other disposals, losses, reliefs and elections. This review covers the six tax years selectable in the app. Independent professional sign-off remains open.

## The differences between the six years

The annual exempt amount (AEA) below is the usual amount for an eligible individual. The two rates are the main individual rates for ordinary gains, with the available basic-rate band determining the split. The proceeds threshold is one rule for people already registered for Self Assessment. Other reasons to report include gains before losses above the applicable AEA and a claim for losses. The threshold is applied to total relevant disposals across the return, before deducting costs. The app covers the accounts and transactions the customer supplied. [HMRC rates and allowances](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/capital-gains-tax-rates-and-allowances) · [HMRC reporting thresholds](https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/work-out-need-to-pay) · [Annual SA108 forms and notes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-capital-gains-summary-sa108)

| UK tax year, 6 April to 5 April | Usual individual AEA | Main ordinary gains rates | Gross proceeds threshold |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2021–22 | £12,300 | 10% / 20% | More than four times the AEA, ordinarily £49,200 |
| 2022–23 | £12,300 | 10% / 20% | More than four times the AEA, ordinarily £49,200 |
| 2023–24 | £6,000 | 10% / 20% | More than £50,000 |
| 2024–25 | £3,000 | 10% / 20% through 29 October 2024, then 18% / 24% from 30 October | More than £50,000 |
| 2025–26 | £3,000 | 18% / 24% | More than £50,000 |
| 2026–27 | £3,000 | 18% / 24% | More than £50,000 |

- **2024–25** has two main rate periods. A sale's date still controls share identification. Retain each dated gain for the filing calculation, including any adjustment HMRC requires. Contracts subject to anti-forestalling provisions require individual review. [HMRC's 2024–25 adjustment guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/work-out-your-capital-gains-tax-adjustment-for-the-2024-to-2025-tax-year)
- **Up to 5 April 2025**, remittance-basis treatment can affect foreign gains, loss claims and the AEA. An earlier transition can give qualifying deemed-domiciled individuals an April 2017 market-value basis. UK residence during the trades alone cannot establish ordinary cost. [HMRC remittance basis](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/remittance-basis-changes) · [HMRC 2017 rebasing](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg25302)
- **From 6 April 2025**, a qualifying new resident can claim foreign income and gains relief. A FIG or Overseas Workday Relief claim removes that year's AEA. A separate rebasing provision can replace historic cost with the 5 April 2017 market value for a qualifying former remittance-basis user disposing of a qualifying foreign asset. The conditions include ownership, location of the asset and the person's prior circumstances. A broker's currency or the domicile implied by an ISIN cannot determine eligibility. Refer these cases for advice. [HMRC FIG eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-can-claim-the-4-year-foreign-income-and-gains-regime) · [FA 2025 Schedule 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/8/schedule/11) · [HMRC AEA exceptions](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/capital-gains-tax-rates-and-allowances)
- **2026–27** is in progress at the review date. A current-year calculation remains provisional. CGT Tracker asks for history through 5 May after the tax year ends before it publishes a complete annual calculation. This is the product's consistent full-year evidence requirement. [HMRC 30-day rule](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51560)

## How each sale is calculated

1. **Identify the owner, share class and contract date.** Combine the same share class across all of that person's taxable broker accounts. A settlement date can put an ordinary trade on the wrong side of 5 April or the 30-day limit. Resolve market and time-zone ambiguity against the execution or contract note. Conditional contracts and changes of ownership or capacity require review. [TCGA 1992 s28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/28) · [HMRC disposal dates](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14250) · [Share identification](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51555)
2. **Match in order.** Aggregate same-day purchases and sales for that class. Reserve those matches first. Match remaining sales to eligible purchases in the next 30 calendar days, then to the Section 104 pool. A qualifying repurchase can fall in the next tax year. Partial matches use proportional acquisition costs, and pool expenditure falls as pooled shares are sold. HMRC also describes a final identification against later purchases where the first three stages are exhausted. The app holds an apparent short or missing ownership history for review. [TCGA 1992 ss104–106A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/part/IV/chapter/I) · [HMRC examples](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51560) · [Pooled expenditure](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51575)
3. **Establish gross values and each fee.** Convert the purchase and sale separately to sterling at the applicable transaction-date exchange rates. Use a reasonable, consistent rate source and retain the source values. Check the direction of the FX rate and the unit of a GBX quote. Allocate evidenced allowable purchase costs to acquisitions and sale costs to disposals exactly once. General custody, subscription and portfolio-management charges fall outside allowable transaction costs. A separate currency conversion or an unclassified foreign charge needs evidence before assigning it to a trade. [TCGA 1992 s38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/38) · [HMRC currency conversion](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg78310) · [HMRC incidental costs](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15250)
4. **Keep the computation and the filing decision distinct.** The engine retains exact pool arithmetic, subtracts exact acquisition costs from net proceeds and rounds the result for display. The annual headline rounds the exact annual totals once. A reconciliation explains any difference from adding the individually displayed disposals. Complete 2024–25 reports also give gains and losses on each side of the rate change, using the exact transactions in each period. Consult the form for the relevant year before transcribing figures, particularly listed/unlisted treatment, losses and claims. Current-year losses and brought-forward losses have different rules. Record the underlying transactions when claiming losses and check the normal four-year claim limit. [SA108 and notes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-capital-gains-summary-sa108) · [HMRC loss relief](https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/losses)

## Cases that change the answer

| Evidence or event | Effect on an ordinary-share calculation |
|---|---|
| Gaps, transfers between brokers, multiple labels or missing accounts | Reconcile ownership and fees to the originating statements. Carry the original acquisition history through a transfer of the same beneficial holding. A missing acquisition can change a pooled cost or identify a sale under the 30-day rule. |
| Private sales, connected parties, gifts, inheritances and spouse/civil partner transfers | Market value, inherited basis, no-gain/no-loss treatment or restrictions on losses can apply. Refer the transaction before using a price paid in cash as its basis. [HMRC share gain calculation](https://www.gov.uk/tax-sell-shares/work-out-your-gain) · [Market-value rule](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14530) |
| Rights, bonus issues, takeovers, splits, capital repayments and changes of share class | The existing cost may carry forward, split between holdings or be reduced. Some issues create no fresh acquisition for the 30-day rule. A cash dividend and a purchase funded by that dividend are separate events. Obtain the corporate-action notice and any supporting valuation. [HMRC reorganisations](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/capital-gains-tax-share-reorganisation-takeover-or-merger) · [HS284](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/shares-and-capital-gains-tax-hs284-self-assessment-helpsheet) |
| Shares held on 31 March 1982, or disposals before 6 April 2008 | The modern pool can require a 1982 valuation or the surviving cost from the earlier matching regime. Post-March-1982 ordinary purchases carried forward untouched generally enter using allowable cost. Continuing holdings affected by an old disposal require reviewed evidence. An evidenced closed legacy holding with the matching window clear need not affect an independent later holding. [HMRC transition](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51570) · [Reviewed opening procedure](REVIEWED_OPENING_POSITIONS.md) |
| ISA/SIPP positions, employee schemes, EIS/SEIS/VCT, funds or ETFs, options, shorts and trading as a business | Personal taxable ordinary shares are the supported investment. Wrapper exemptions, employment income/basis, reliefs, offshore income gains, equalisation or excess reportable income, derivatives rules or income-tax treatment can take a case outside that scope. Classify the instrument from broker evidence. [HMRC shares](https://www.gov.uk/tax-sell-shares) · [Share reliefs](https://www.gov.uk/tax-sell-shares/relief) · [Offshore funds](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/offshore-funds-self-assessment-helpsheet-hs265/hs265-offshore-funds) · [Individual financial traders](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim56850) |

## Results of this review

- **Year-end purchase missing.** With 10 shares bought for £100, all 10 sold for £200 on 5 April and a further unexplained sale of 10 on 7 April, the previous engine published £100. The new engine withholds the affected disposal cost. Supplying the actual 6 April purchase for £180 gives a £20 gain. Tests distinguish partial repurchases, days 30 and 31 and unrelated securities. [Engine regression](../../tests/cgt.test.ts)
- **External monetary benchmark.** HMRC's worked example has two purchases, four dealing charges and two pooled sales. With full-precision pooling the report shows gains of £329.33 and £300.33, consistent with the £329 and £300 gains in HMRC's whole-pound illustration. Its exact annual gain rounds to £629.67, with £629.66 obtained by adding the two displayed lines. The test uses February 2026 for the second sale, following the printed calculation in Step 4. [HMRC worked example](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69c14ad87e02b81c0d1c7669/HS284-Example-3-2026.pdf) · [Reference tests](../../tests/hmrc-reference.test.ts)
- **Independent arithmetic comparison.** An acquisition-oriented exact-rational comparator checked 4,200 additional generated long-only portfolios across the supported years. The 152,576 transactions produced 45,811 grouped disposals with zero differences on the tested matching allocations, pool quantities and costs, rounded disposals, annual gross proceeds and fees, exact annual headline amounts and the sums of displayed lines. For 1,339 complete 2024–25 portfolios the exact gains, losses and net in both rate periods also agreed. This exercise starts with valid sterling transactions. Broker interpretation, tax elections and undocumented holdings need separate evidence. [Version-controlled numerical model tests](../../tests/cgt-model.test.ts) · [Broker support evidence](BROKER_SUPPORT.md)
- **Import through to the delivered figures.** A six-trade Trading 212 file closes a pooled holding and realises gains and losses on both sides of 30 October 2024. Its unrounded annual net is £1.005, reported as £1.01. The displayed period nets are £1.67 and −£0.67. PostgreSQL qualification checks the confirmed and draft CSV, summary PDF and evidence PDF, including the reconciliation and the omission of final rate-period totals on the draft.
